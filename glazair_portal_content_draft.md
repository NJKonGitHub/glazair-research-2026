## 02 — The Platform

### A digital layer for a fragmented industry

Automotive glass servicing today runs largely on phone calls, emails, and manual coordination. There is no standard digital workflow, no structured way for vehicle operators to find and compare qualified providers, and no consistent process from damage report to completed job.

Glazair exists to fill that gap — not by replacing what workshops do or changing how they operate, but by adding a new demand channel to boost their business and let them concentrate on what they do best: delivering excellent glass repair and replacement work.

### How it works

The closest analogy is Booking.com — in the sense that Glazair aggregates demand from multiple sources and digitalises the process end to end. But the similarity ends there.

There are no fixed prices. There is no simple listing. Every job that enters the platform becomes a request for quotation, distributed to relevant workshops based on geography and capability. You decide whether to respond, on what terms, and at what price.

### Where the work comes from

Jobs enter the platform from three sources: commercial vehicle operators managing their own fleets, businesses with smaller vehicle needs, and private vehicle owners. Each source follows the same digital workflow — a structured damage report, automatic glass identification where applicable, and an RFQ distributed to qualified workshops in the relevant area.

Not every job reaches every workshop. Routing is based on relevance — geography, capability, and over time, performance. If a job is not relevant to you, it simply does not appear.

### Your participation, your terms

Nothing on Glazair is mandatory. You choose which requests to respond to and which to pass on. There is no penalty for not quoting — however we would love to see you as an active partner, responding to as many requests as possible. We will reward your commitment and loyalty.

There is no obligation to accept work outside your capacity or preference.

The platform brings the opportunity. What you do with it is entirely up to you.

### The role division

Glazair acquires the customer, manages the digital workflow, handles customer communication, and coordinates the process from request to completion. You perform the work.

You invoice Glazair — not the end customer. That means no separate customer acquisition effort, no chasing payment from multiple counterparties, and no administrative overhead beyond the job itself.

### What the platform does — and does not — do

For replacement jobs, Glazair identifies the primary glass component and provides its OE part number and specification. That part of the identification work is handled for you.

What the platform does not determine is how the job is executed. The professional responsibility for the repair or replacement — including any additional components, materials, tooling, and compliance with manufacturer repair technology — remains entirely with the workshop. Your quote must reflect all foreseeable costs. Once accepted, it is binding.

## 03 — Roles & Responsibilities

### What Glazair takes care of

Glazair manages everything on the customer-facing side of the job: acquiring the customer, handling communication throughout the process, orchestrating the request and offer workflow, managing disputes where they arise, and maintaining the digital infrastructure that holds it all together.

When a job enters the platform, Glazair also performs the primary glass component identification — providing the OE part number and specification for replacement jobs. That step is handled before the request reaches you.

### What the workshop takes care of

You perform the work. That means the full professional responsibility for how the job is executed: determining any additional components, materials, consumables, and tooling required; ensuring compliance with manufacturer repair and replacement technology; and delivering the service to the standard the customer expects.

Depending on the nature of the job, Glazair may ask the workshop to carry out specific administrative tasks required by its business customers — for example, photographic documentation of the damage or completed work, or filling in job-related forms. Where this applies, it will be clearly communicated with the job.

The platform provides the job. The craft is yours.

### The sticker

For eligible jobs, the workshop is responsible for affixing a Glazair-branded sticker to the vehicle upon completion. Stickers are provided by Glazair. The sticker carries a unique QR code that registers the vehicle in the platform and enables future service requests to be handled digitally. Placement instructions are included with the sticker pack.

### The invoicing model

You invoice Glazair. Not the fleet operator, not the vehicle owner — Glazair.

This means one counterparty for all platform-generated work, predictable payment terms, and no administrative burden of managing receivables across multiple clients. Whatever the source of the job, your commercial relationship is always with Glazair.

### The liability boundary

Glazair's responsibility ends where the physical work begins. The platform does not prescribe repair technology, does not specify tooling or consumables, and does not validate technical compliance. These are professional judgements that belong with the workshop.

This boundary exists to protect both sides. Glazair does not interfere with how you work. In return, the workshop carries full responsibility for the quality and safety of the service delivered.

## 04 — Quoting

### How quoting works

When a relevant job reaches you, you receive a structured request containing everything you need to prepare an offer: vehicle details, damage description, the primary glass component specification for replacement jobs, location, and any specific requirements attached to the job.

You review the request and decide whether to submit an offer. If you do, your offer specifies your price, availability, and where applicable, whether calibration is included.

### The reference price

Every job on the platform carries a reference price. This is not a fictitious or theoretical figure — it is calculated from actual market data including current purchase prices and prevailing labour rates, and it represents a price at which real capacity exists in the market to carry out that specific job.

The reference price also reflects the payment context of the job. The same repair can carry a different reference price depending on whether the cost is borne directly by the vehicle owner or covered by an insurer. This is mirrored in the figure you see, so you are always working with a price that reflects the actual commercial context of the job.

Since Glazair expects to receive offers that improve on the reference price, it should be understood as a benchmark rather than a target. What constitutes a strong offer depends on the customer — some weigh price most heavily, others prioritise availability, calibration, or service mode. Workshops who perform well across multiple dimensions will consistently be well positioned.

### Your offer is binding

Once submitted, your offer can be adjusted until the RFQ window closes. After that point, your offer enters a 15-day validity period during which it can be accepted or declined by the customer — but cannot be changed or withdrawn.

If your offer is awarded within that 15-day window, it becomes binding until the service is fulfilled, for a maximum of 30 days from the award date.

This means the workshop carries the risk of any changes that occur between offer submission and job completion. Parts pricing, supplier availability, and sourcing conditions can shift in that window. If a supplier confirms availability at a given price but cannot deliver — and an alternative supplier charges more — that difference is absorbed by the workshop, not passed to the customer.

These risks are best managed by scheduling the job at the earliest possible date.

### How decisions are made

Price matters, but it is not the only factor. Customers weigh a range of elements when selecting an offer: availability and turnaround time, mobile or in-workshop execution, component quality, calibration inclusion, and distance. Over time, workshop performance metrics will also play a role.

A competitive price is an advantage. So is a strong service proposition.

## 05 — Getting Started

### The easiest way in

Most workshops do not run active marketing or customer acquisition for private vehicle owners. These jobs arrive occasionally through word of mouth or walk-ins — welcome when they come, but not something most operations invest in generating.

On Glazair, private vehicle jobs are a natural starting point. They arrive through the platform's B2C channel, they carry no complexity around fleet authorisation or corporate workflows, and they represent demand that is largely additional to what the workshop already has. For many partners, this is where the relationship with Glazair begins — seeing what comes in, responding to what fits, and building familiarity with the workflow at a pace that suits them.

### No large commitment required

There is no requirement to dedicate capacity, hit volume targets, or restructure how the workshop operates. Glazair works alongside existing operations. The simplest way to think about it: an additional digital channel that you monitor, respond to when it makes sense, and grow into over time.

### Performance visibility

As jobs are completed through the platform, you gain access to data that is currently invisible to most workshops: your win rate on submitted offers, your average response speed, and over time, how your performance compares to the broader market.

This is not a ranking or a public scorecard. It is operational intelligence — useful for understanding where you are strong and where there may be room to improve.

## 06 — Questions & Concerns

### Will Glazair create a price war among workshops?

No. Glazair does not rank offers by price alone, and customers do not see a simple price list. Every award decision weighs a combination of factors — availability, service mode, component quality, calibration inclusion, distance, and over time, performance metrics. A competitive price is an advantage, but it is one input among several. Workshops that deliver consistently strong service across multiple dimensions will be well positioned regardless of whether they are the lowest-priced option.

### What happens to my existing customers — will Glazair compete with them?

No. Glazair does not market to or contact your existing customers. The platform generates new demand from sources that are not currently in your pipeline — fleet operators, corporate clients, and private vehicle owners who find Glazair through its own channels. Your existing relationships are entirely unaffected.

### Who owns the customer relationship?

Glazair is the contracting party to the end customer. This is a deliberate design choice: it protects the workshop from direct customer liability and administrative complexity, and it allows Glazair to maintain a consistent customer experience across the network. In practice, the customer interacts with Glazair's workflow and brand throughout. Your workshop delivers the service.

### What if I win a job but cannot deliver?

If an awarded job cannot be fulfilled, a penalty of 30% of the offer value is payable to Glazair. Offers are binding commitments — this applies to all awarded jobs regardless of the reason for non-delivery. The best protection against this is accurate quoting and early scheduling.

## 07 — Commercial Terms

### Commission

Glazair charges a commission of 6% on the value of each completed job. This is deducted at settlement.

If your response rate to relevant RFQ requests exceeds 90% in a given settlement month, 1% of the commission is refunded — reducing your effective rate to 5% for that month. This is our way of recognising and rewarding active partnership.

### Settlement

At the end of each calendar month, Glazair issues a settlement report itemising all completed jobs, their values, and the commission applied. You issue your invoice against this report. Your invoice must reconcile with the report — Glazair will not accept invoices that do not match. If there is a discrepancy, the report is corrected and reissued first, after which you resubmit your invoice against the agreed figures. Payment is made within 20 days of invoice acceptance.

Insurance jobs requiring additional administrative work carry a flat-rate administrative compensation of 30,000 HUF per job, settled as a separate line item within the same monthly cycle and invoice.

### Non-compliance

If a job is awarded to your workshop and you fail to deliver, a penalty of 30% of the offer value is payable to Glazair. Offers are binding commitments — this penalty exists to ensure that awarded jobs are treated as such.

### Sticker attribution commission

When you affix a Glazair sticker to a vehicle, you earn an attribution commission of 300 HUF per new vehicle registered. In addition, if that vehicle completes any job through the Glazair platform within two years of sticker assignment, you earn 1% of that job's value — regardless of which workshop carries out the work.

Commercial rates are set per market and may differ between geographies.
